# Research Figures

Static figures for the six research areas on the lab homepage.

- Platform, computational-design, and proposed AI-design figures come from Luyang Zhao's research statement and the associated projects.
- Tensegrity-Blocks construction and ground-docking images: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60982-0
- ASCII-VLA manipulation frames are excerpts of the original Figure 6: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.21470
- `proj4.png` illustrates the PLRC motion-planning work.

Captions distinguish published demonstrations, proposed research, and ongoing work. The shelter fabric was human-placed, and the aerial camera for ground docking was manually positioned.
